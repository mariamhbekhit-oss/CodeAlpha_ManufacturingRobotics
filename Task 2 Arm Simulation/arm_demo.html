2R robotic arm: pick and place

Two independent rotary joints · 250 mm + 180 mm links · ideal planar kinematics

What to observe

The blue links move to the pick fixture. The orange object follows the tool while the grip is closed. After release, the object remains at the place fixture as the arm returns home.

This viewer plays the Python-generated trajectory. It is not a physical robot, torque simulation or collision check. Tool orientation is not independently controlled. Open Simulation_Report.pdf for equations and test results.
